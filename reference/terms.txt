7) Booking, Cancellation & Lateness​
Cancellation or rescheduling with at least 24 hours notice: no charge.
Cancellation or rescheduling between 1 and 24 hours before the session: 50% of the session fee is charged.
Cancellation less than 1 hour before the session, or a no-show: 100% of the session fee is charged.
Extenuating circumstances are reviewed case by case.
Student lateness: the teacher waits up to 10 minutes; the session still ends at the scheduled time.
Yeda lateness/cancellation: if we are late by more than 10 minutes or must cancel, we will reschedule at no cost or credit the equivalent session.
Customer-side technical issues: if the Student is unable to participate due to a Customer-side technical issue while the teacher remains available and reasonably attempts to establish an alternative connection for at least 10 minutes, the applicable late-cancellation or no-show charge may apply.
Yeda-side technical issues: if a session cannot proceed due to a Yeda-side technical issue or platform-wide outage, no charge applies. Any prepaid entitlement is restored and a replacement session will be offered.
