5.1) The Yeda Student Card
What it is:
an internal tool that brings together information about a Student's learning journey — teacher post-lesson feedback, parent input, student self-reflections, and grade updates — into one organized overview. It helps our team see the full picture and track progress over time.
Participation is optional.
Every Customer is asked whether they want to participate via a standalone consent form. Declining has no effect on the quality of teaching or any other aspect of our service.
Use of AI:
the Student Card uses AI tools to organize and summarize the information described above. Before any AI processing, directly identifying information (names, contact details, address) is removed or masked. The AI's role is strictly limited to organizing information into an overview and flagging pre-defined occurrences for human attention (for example, a drop in grades or a student expressing dissatisfaction with lessons). The AI does not evaluate the Student, make recommendations, or make any decisions. All conclusions and any adjustments to a Student's learning plan are made by our team — qualified humans, led by our learning specialist.
No automated decision-making:
we do not use the Student Card (or any other tool) to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on Students. The Student Card is a support tool for human judgment.
No health or special-category data:
the Student Card does not contain medical or health-related diagnoses or other special-category data under Article 9 GDPR. Where the learning specialist contributes input, it takes the form of educational best-practice guidance on how to work with the Student, not clinical or health information.
​
Use & sharing:
the Student Card is used internally by Yeda to inform our team's work and periodic updates shared with the Customer. Teachers receive only selected outputs relevant to their work, not the full card. It is never published, shared with advertisers, or used for any commercial purpose beyond delivering our service.
Legal basis:
consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), collected from the Customer (parent/guardian for minors) via a standalone consent form. Consent can be withdrawn at any time; upon withdrawal, we stop the processing and delete the Student Card data (subject to legal retention obligations for business records).
Retention:
up to 12 months after last activity, or earlier upon request or withdrawal of consent.
